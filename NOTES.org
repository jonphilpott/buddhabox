*** Speaker Recommendations

Very good, sir. Let me run the numbers on both candidates against your
0.52L sealed enclosure.

Dayton Audio CE48-4 (~$8)
  - Fs: 151 Hz, Vas: 0.068L, Qts: 0.95, Xmax: 1.2mm, Sensitivity: 79 dB
  - In your 0.52L box: Qtc ≈ 1.01 — heavily overdamped, will sound
    bloated and boomy

  Dayton Audio CE52N-4 (~$8)
  - Fs: 164 Hz, Vas: 0.17L, Qts: 0.59, Xmax: 0.85mm, Sensitivity: 85
    dB
  - In your 0.52L box: Qtc ≈ 0.68 — very close to the ideal
    Butterworth alignment of 0.707. Flat, well-controlled bass.

  The CE52N-4 is, if I may say so, a rather pleasing stroke of luck —
  it is almost textbook-optimally matched to your enclosure. It is
  also 6 dB more sensitive than the CE48-4, meaning it will sound
  noticeably louder for the same amplifier output, which matters given
  the MAX98357A's modest power output.

  There is one rather agreeable coincidence: its system -3dB point in
  your box works out to approximately 185 Hz, which aligns almost
  perfectly with the output_highpass_freq = 180.0f we just set. The
  DSP and the driver will be rolling off together rather than fighting
  each other. Most satisfactory.
